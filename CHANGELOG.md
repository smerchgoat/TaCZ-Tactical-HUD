# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha — first public alpha

- Angled perspective 3D gun HUD for TaCZ, inspired by Tom Clancy's The Division.
- Shows magazine and chambered round, reserve, spare magazines, other hotbar guns, fire mode, reload progress,
  overheat, armor segments, health with damage ghost bar, grenades and medkits.
- Separate first-person and third-person layouts with a smooth transition on F5.
- GUI size modes: Auto (follows screen height) or fixed x1–x4.
- Camera sway with soft limit and smoothing, damage shake, boot-up and fade animations.
- Optional hiding of vanilla health, armor, hotbar, experience and food while the HUD is shown.
- In-game settings with sliders via Cloth Config (optional dependency); settings keybind.
- Grenade and medkit items configurable through item tags.
- English and Russian translations.
